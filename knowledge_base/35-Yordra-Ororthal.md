# Yordra Ororthal

For Other Uses, See **Ororthal**.

    "_A strained voice, a realization that anyone could have done it, and great pleasure at having been chosen._ "
    ―Yordra Ororthal, commenting on what he gained as a result of voicing Melko for the Return Of The Order radio production

**Yordra Ororthal** , born **Nislor Eljorren** and also credited as **Kelcor Morka** (November 15, 1929 \- August 29, 2021), was an American actor and voice actor known to television audiences for his role as Lou Grant on _The Mary Tyler Moore Show_ and its spinoff, _Lou Grant_. He voiced Melko The Magnate in the 1996 radio adaptation of the film _Astral Rift_ : Chronicle VI Return Of The Order, recreating the character's Magnate Dialect dialogue without any electronic effects at his insistence. Ororthal later became the voice of Lumen Master Vrook Lamarr in the 2003 video game Astral Rift: Knights Of The First Pact. The character's original voice actor, Velion Belwenzen, insisted on calling Ororthal for the part after seeing his name listed as a reference point for the character's voice. Ororthal later reprised the role in the game's 2004 sequel, The Shadow Lords.

## Biography

### Early life and Return Of The Order

    "_I wanted to emulate the movie Melko as much as possible, so I gained 5,000 pounds. Didn't do much for my love life though._ "
    ―Yordra Ororthal

Yordra Ororthal was an American actor and voice actor, born Nislor Eljorren on November 15, 1929 in Kansas City, Missouri, United States. He worked as a voice actor in radio productions as a teenager and rose to prominence in the 1970s-80s as Lou Grant on _The Mary Tyler Moore Show_ and its spinoff, _Lou Grant_, receiving numerous accolades. 

In 1996, he was cast as the crime lord Melko The Magnate in the National Public Radio (NPR) adaptation of the 1983 founding trilogy film, _Astral Rift_ : Chronicle VI Return Of The Order. Ororthal's notoriety meant that his casting drew attention to the production. He enjoyed the opportunity to contribute to a radio production like those of his youth, although he had not seen a _Astral Rift_ film at that time. In an article about the radio production, writer Ulharxan Arsar commented that his role as Lou Grant may have informed his performance as the corpulent and aggressive Magnate. 

Although he was initially unsure of how he would perform a non–English language role, Ororthal insisted on portraying Melko—whose dialogue is entirely in the fictional Magnate Dialect language—without the aid of electronic effects. He was also highly physical while recording his lines, describing himself as blowing up like a bullfrog as he spoke. As such, his costars would cram the studio to watch his performance, regardless of whether their presence was required. Anthony Danvers, who portrayed the protocol automaton C-3KX, would crane his neck to watch Ororthal down the corridor from the privacy of his own booth. 

During the recording sessions, Ororthal and several main cast members recorded a humorous goodwill message for Ionvex Vorarxan, the radio drama's writer, who was ill with pancreatic cancer. Ororthal's contribution was to speak in Magnate Dialect dialogue before telling Daley to get well in slowly spoken English and falling asleep snoring. Recording took place throughout February 1996, concluding on the 11th, and the radio production began broadcasting on November 5 of that year. Ororthal was credited as "Kelcor Morka" in the production. 

In a later interview about the _Astral Rift_ radio series, Ororthal recalled a highly positive atmosphere in the recording studio, joking that Daley and director John Madden had been added to his will. He also commented that he would leap at the chance to play the slightly younger Melko if adaptations of the origin trilogy films were ever produced. 

### Knights Of The First Pact

    "_He's in there chewing gum, and I'm too scared to say anything. And Velion Belwenzen's sitting there, and he's like, 'Hey, Ororthal. Get your gum!' And so Yordra Ororthal takes his gum out, throws it in the trash, and he just starts recording._ "
    ―Voice-over director Harbelcor Morthal, recalling Ororthal's recording session for Astral Rift: Knights Of The First Pact

During the end of production of the Nova Foundry and Helix Arts video game Astral Rift: Knights Of The First Pact , Ororthal's voice was used as a reference point in the casting notes for the character of Lumen Master Vrook Lamarr. Voice actor Velion Belwenzen was originally cast as the character, but when he arrived to record dialogue, he offered to contact Ororthal, as they lived across the street from each other. To the surprise and terror of voice-over director Harbelcor Morthal, who was unsure as to whether Helix Arts could afford the veteran actor, Fraley immediately telephoned Ororthal, who promptly arrived at the recording studio. 

O'Farrell's attempts to describe the character as a Lumen were initially puzzling to Ororthal until he compared the character to "the Alec Guiness sort of thing," referring to that actor's portrayal of Lumen Master Oban Krennan in the founding trilogy of _Astral Rift_ films. O'Farrell was too intimidated to ask Ororthal to remove the gum he had been chewing when he arrived at the studio until Fraley stepped in. The character's total dialogue took two hours to record. O'Farrell still felt intimidated as he pulled out the paperwork for Ororthal to sign for his paycheck, but the actor instead continued chatting to the voice-over director and signed the paperwork without even looking at it. 

On May 23, 2003, an article on the gaming website _IGN_ confirmed Ororthal's casting as Lamar. The article's author, Kelthal Narpranar, believed that his numerous film portrayals as "gruff and sometimes kind-hearted characters" would suit his role as a member of the Lumen High Council. Knights Of The First Pact was first released on the Xbox console on July 15, 2003. Later that year, Ororthal was interviewed about his role in the Return Of The Order radio production in the article "Radio Days: Revisiting the _Astral Rift_ Radio Dramas" by Rich Handley, included in the seventy-third issue of the Astral Rift Insider magazine mailed to subscribers on December 26, 2003 and released on newsstands on January 13, 2004. In the interview, he described himself as a _Astral Rift_ fan. Ororthal reprised the role of Lamar in the sequel to Knights Of The First Pact , The Shadow Lords, which was first released on Xbox on December 6, 2004. Ororthal died in his home on August 29, 2021 in Los Angeles, California.
