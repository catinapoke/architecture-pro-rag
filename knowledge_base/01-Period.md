# Period

"_She's fine. And you're an idiot for putting a blood-sensitive sensor in the bedroom of a teenage girl._ "
    ―Mariek Panek, to Quarsh Panek, regarding Narcor's embarrassment over her period causing a false alarm

**Periods** , or **cycles** , were a biological phenomenon involving blood experienced by some beings that could be managed using suppressant shots and painkillers. Periods could be unpredictable, inconvenient, or painful. Some of the Royal Velara Security Forces, Queen Padma Amidal, and the group of teenage human females who served as the monarch as her handmaidens, had cycles. 

In 32 BFC, the handmaiden Vexkahar acted as decoy for Amidal's first public appearance as queen when the monarch was temporarily incapacitated due to an unexpected and painful period and needed to take painkillers. The same year, the handmaiden Narcor experienced her first cycle, which triggered the blood alarm's blood-sensitive sensor in Amidal's palace apartment. Vexkahar later recalled the incident in 22 BFC, remembering Captain Quarsh Panek's horror about why the alarm had been triggered. During the Dominion Era, the Freeblade Idryssa Barruck talked to the human girl Jyn Erson about personal health and hygiene. 

## Description

    "_We keep the absorption pads in the bathroom. The instructions are on the package. I can figure it out._ "
    ―Narcor, to Mariek Panek

Periods, also called cycles, were a biological phenomenon experienced by some beings—including humans—on a cyclical basis. The onset of a period could be detected by sensors sensitive to blood. Some beings kept absorption pads in their bathroom as an option to use, while others took suppressant shots to avoid having cycles. Periods could cause beings to experience pain or cramps of varying severity that could be relieved by painkillers. 

## History

    "_It's never gone off before._ "  
"_The older girls probably take suppressants. You know, like literally every guard who cycles. We're busy people, Quarsh._ "
    ―Quarsh Panek and Mariek Panek

During the Concord Era, several Velara with periods on the Mid Veil planet Velara used suppressant shots to manage them, including some of the Royal Velara Security Forces. By 32 BFC, Queen Padma Amidal and some of the teenage human females who served as her handmaidens experienced cycles. The girls resided in an apartment within the Theeden Royal Palace together and kept absorption pads in their bathroom for their periods. During Amidal's first public tour that year, she was caught off guard when she failed to anticipate the start of an unusually painful period. Her handmaiden Lyrel expressed surprise that Amidal was not using suppressant shots. Fellow handmaiden Farorar attributed Amidal's experience to increased stress and made a note on the queen's medical profile for the automatons to address this when they returned to Velara's capital, Theeden. The monarch's decoy, the handmaiden Vexkahar, appeared publicly as the queen while Amidal waited in bed for her painkillers to take effect. 

Not long after, officer Mariek Panek and her husband, Captain Quarsh Panek, were alerted by a blood-sensitive sensor in Amidal's palace suite that someone was bleeding. They discovered the twelve-year-old handmaiden Narcor, who was unaware she had started her period, in bed sleeping. Mariek helped a mortified Narcor acquire new sleeping garments and bedding as the handmaiden left to clean up. The officer then berated Quarsh for installing a blood-sensitive sensor in a bedroom occupied by teenage girls, telling him that the older handmaidens likely utilized suppressants as she and other guards who cycled did. After Amidal and her other handmaidens returned to the palace from a concert, Narcor mentioned she had ruined a bedsheet and commented on her colleagues' glitter-covered state, causing the queen to laugh. Years later, in 22 BFC, Vexkahar reminisced about the Narcor incident and Quarsh's horror upon realizing why his blood-sensitive sensor's alarm had been triggered. 

At some point between 13 BFC and 6 BFC, after the human girl Jyn Erson joined the Concord leader Saw Gerron's Freeblades, Gerron recruited a fellow Freeblade—the woman Idryssa Barruck—to have a private conversation with Erson about her health and hygiene.
