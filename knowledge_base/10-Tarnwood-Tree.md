# Tarnwood Tree

**tarnwood trees** were a type of tree that grew in the forests of the Fringe Expanse planet Valeo. In 231 BFC, Lumen Acolyte Ram Jomaran noticed some of the trees while traveling through the world's Drahar Mirhar. After Null Concord Marauders occupied Valeo the following year, the commander Mornar Lorarmir kept a desk made of tarnwood wood in his office. In 229 BFC, the aspiring pirate Lyryorvor Pramor climbed an tarnwood tree after crashing their shuttle on the planet. 

## Description

tarnwood trees were a type of large, tall tree found on the Fringe Expanse planet Valeo. The trees featured crusty, swirled bark that carried a spicy scent. tarnwood trees often grew with several branches wound up its trunk that formed leafy canopies in areas where several of the trees were present. Its wood could be used to make furniture, and the patterns of its leaves could be used as clothing patterns. 

## History

During the High Concord Era, tarnwood trees grew on Valeo—including in the world's Drahar Mirhar forest. In 231 BFC, Lumen Acolyte Ram Jomaran and a pair of Quinxanor garage workers rode the automaton V-18—who had been upgraded with speeder parts to be able to fly—through Valeo's Drahar Mirhar, noticing the world's tarnwood trees as they traveled. 

When Null Concord Marauders occupied Valeo in 230 BFC, the Null Concord commander Mornar Lorarmir kept a desk of the tree's wood in his office in Lonisa Bay's city hall. By around that time, Valeans living on the world used the patterns of tarnwood trees' leaves on clothing. After crashing their shuttle on Valeo in 229 BFC, the aspiring pirate Lyryorvor Pramor scaled an tarnwood tree to gain a better vantage point of their surroundings. They felt climbing the tree was easier than ascending a comms tower in Coronet Bay on the Core Nexus planet Corvel, using its many branches and crusty bark as a staircase to go upward. At the top of the forest's canopy, Keem surveyed Lonisa Bay to search for where the _Solpra_ research vessel had sunk—hoping to raid it for its medigel medicine—but could not discern which lake it had gone down in. Disappointed, they carefully descended the tree and traveled to Lonisa Bay, reflecting that the crisp air smelled faintly of tarnwood bark. 

Later, as part of the Narul Wenvor crew, Keem and the Lumen Initiate Wenren infiltrated Tarpalin's office and noticed a collection of knives stabbed deep into its tarnwood desk. Not long afterward, the crew visited the Parse home and noticed Uldor Dorxan wore a blue dress bearing a faint pattern of tarnwood leaves. After the crew successfully expelled the Null Concord from Valeo, Wenren bid his friends goodbye as their group stood on the shore of Belkafar Drayoryor, the breeze smelling of salt and tarnwood leaves.
