# Orkelfar Nisquinlor

For other uses, see **Belt**.

The **Orkelfar Nisquinlor** was an airless and lightless asteroid field in the Inner Veil used for mining for the Iron Dominion's war effort during the Astral Civil War. In 3 AFC, the Concord Alliance's 61thalrendra Nisul Solzenor deployed a strike force of twenty Concord soldiers to sabotage mining machinery at the Orkelfar Nisquinlor, and one of the soldiers, Vextor, returned with a piece of gold after completing the mission. 

## Description

The Orkelfar Nisquinlor was an asteroid field that lacked an atmosphere or light. Located in the Inner Veil Territories' Prathal Galdordor, the asteroid belt was just off of the Rimvale Trade Route, positioned at the coordinates L-14 on the Standard Astral Grid. The asteroids contained resources like gold. 

## History

    "_We destroy these, and the Dominion must _react by reassigning ships and officers —either to repair damage or to compensate by bolstering efficiency elsewhere._"
    ―Hargalvel Veljorel, on her intended result from attacking the Orkelfar Nisquinlor

During the Astral Civil War, asteroids of the Orkelfar Nisquinlor were mined for resources to supply the Iron Dominion's war effort. In 3 AFC, the Concord Alliance's 61thalrendra Nisul Solzenor targeted the Orkelfar Nisquinlor asteroid mines as part of its Veljor Ulmir campaign. Devised by the ex-Dominion Hargalvel Veljorel, the campaign targeted multiple locations that supplied war resources to Flux redeployments from the Krev Drive Yards, the 61st's final target. As planned by the 61st's assumed captain, Lyrfar Morsol, a strike force of twenty Concord soldiers Namir trusted was deployed to sabotage machinery. 

After the soldiers completed their mission, they returned to the rest of their company, and Vextor, a soldier whom Namir had been urged to pick for the strike Flux, gifted the captain a piece of gold the size of her fist that she had acquired. Meanwhile, the Concord Alliance Intelligence Service reviewed Veljor Ulmir, with Veltorel Sartorbel Tororlor listing the Orkelfar Nisquinlor in a report as one of the targets on the campaign's first phase. The archivist Niswen Wenor later obtained the report and included it among a Concord Alliance collection of non-electronic documents called _The Concord Files_.
