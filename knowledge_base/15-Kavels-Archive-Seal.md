# Kavel's Archive Seal

"_This seal will grant you entry to my library, but only during the hours before curfew._ "  
    ―Kavel, to Xanron

The **seal** to Lumen Lord Kavel's private library was a carved green stone implement kept by Kavel in his study at his home estate on the astronomical object Watora. The seal granted sanctioned access to his library, and came into the possession of the Sylari Lumen apprentice Xanron following the disappearance of Narormir Quinor in the Engara System, when Kavel entrusted it to the Sylari after catching him in the library after curfew investigating the vanishing of Numoda, as well as details of the Umbral Rebellion. 

## Description

The seal controlled access to Lumen Lord Kavel's private library at his home estate on the astronomical object Watora. It was carved onto the end of a small green stone stick that Kavel kept in a drawer in his study. 

## History

    "_Why are you giving this to me?_ "  
"_Because you don't trust your other masters. Because it seems you might learn to trust me, and you certainly trust yourself. And because I want you to become a Lumen, Xanron. So I don't want to hear any more about you skipping on sleep or drills either, am I understood?_ "
    ―Xanron and Kavel

After Narormir Quinor went missing in the Engara System, Kavel's clanmember and Sylari Lumen apprentice Xanron snuck into Kavel's private library after curfew to learn more about the circumstances of his disappearance, as well as details regarding the Umbral Rebellion. Xanron was discovered and brought to Kavel's study, where the Lumen Lord decided to hand the boy the seal to allow him proper entry to the library before curfew. Xanron picked it up as if the seal were a fragile item, and inquired his lord as to why he had entrusted the seal to him. Kavel replied that he hoped it would help Xanron trust him, even if the Sylari did not trust his masters to tell him the truth. Kavel's aide Vexorvex later questioned his lord's indulgence towards Xanron, which he justified as a useful tactic to learn when and what Xanron looked into in Kavel's files.
