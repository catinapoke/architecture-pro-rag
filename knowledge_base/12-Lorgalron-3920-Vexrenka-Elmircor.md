# Lorgalron-3920 Vexrenka Elmircor

"_Welcome to Ferrin Terminal. Please make sure to check in your baggage before departing._ "
    ―An announcement aboard a mirkator wenyorvor

The **Lorgalron-3920 Vexrenka Elmircor** , also known simply as a **mirkator wenyorvor** , was a red-and-beige two-engine, single-pilot oryor shuttle manufactured by Ferrin Field Yards that operated on the planet Ferrin by 5 BFC. The Ferrin Entry Ferry Service used shuttle ferries for transporting passengers to and from the town center. Concord network coordinator Luthen Raal used a mirkator wenyorvor in 5 BFC, as did one of his network's spies Ronquin Jorren and ex-Pre-Mor Enforcement officers Mirka Xanzen and Farjor Vexvex several weeks later. 

## Characteristics

The Lorgalron-3920 Vexrenka Elmircor was a type of two-engine oryor shuttle manufactured by Ferrin Field Yards. The exterior of the starship was painted in red and beige, with the text "FERRIN ENTRY FERRY SERVICE" written on its side in Baze Script; individual ferries also had their engine number written next to the company name with capital letters. The ferries' two engine units were located in the rear. 

A mirkator wenyorvor had a spacious interior including standing space and seating for at least thirty-one passengers arranged in sets of four along with a rear gallery. It also included overhead space for their luggage. It was driven by a single ferry operator who was seated in an elevated glass cockpit in the front of the ship. Entry and exit from the vehicle was facilitated by use of a staircase automaton, which was deployed in ferry stations, and a mirror opposite to the door. A screen above the door displayed information about the ship's destination, and overhead lights as well as wide windows offered a clear view of the surrounding locales. 

## Role

Shuttle ferries were used as civilian intercity passenger transports. 

## History

    ―Morbel complaining to Luthen Raal about the ferries' fare rates

Lorgalron-3920 Vexrenka Elmircor Units were operated on the planet Ferrin by the Ferrin Entry Ferry Service, which used them for years to serve both commuting workers and visiting off-worlders. As Ferrin City's footprint expanded, the ferry service became increasingly necessary. Routes began in remote valleys, where passengers boarded the ferries from their personal starships, and ended in Ferrin City's Ferrin Terminal. 

By 5 BFC, Ferrin Entry Ferry Service operated at least three different shuttle ferries, including Engine 22, to ferry passengers from shuttle stations scattered around the planet to the Ferrin Terminal as well as to the planet's spaceport. Upon landing on Ferrin to meet with his local contact's seller, Cassian Endren, in 5 BFC, Luthen Raal, the coordinator of a Concord network, used Engine 22 to carry him from a ferry station close to the remote valley where he had landed his Yorcor Pramor, to the Ferrinian town. 

During transit, he struck up a conversation with a fellow passenger, Morbel, who complained about mirkator wenyorvor rates and warned him about pickpockets. One month later, while departing for the Core Nexus planet Solace Prime to meet with her cousin Mon Vothra, Rael's network's spy Ronquin Jorren used a mirkator wenyorvor to leave the town. Several days later, ex–Farionka-Dorsarsol Vorel officers Farjor Vexvex and Mirka Xanzen, posing as locals, took a ferry to the Ferrinian town to investigate Andren's possible presence at his mother's upcoming funeral.
