# Korrath Alloy Heavy Suit

The **Korrath Alloy Heavy Suit** or **Korrath-alloy Armor** was a type of light armor that saw use in 3956 BFC, during the Lumen Civil War. The armor was created by the emerging society on the Fringe Expanse planet Korrath and was well-known for its strength. Along with other small arms, the armor was exported to fund Korrath's exploration of the galaxy. It was constructed with a unique alloy created on the port world, and was owned by several merchants throughout the galaxy. The armor continued to be available several years later in 3951 BFC, during the Shadow Wars, when it was worn by Admiral Carth Orlen. 

## Description

The Korrath Alloy Heavy Suit, also called Korrath-alloy Armor, was constructed using Korrath Alloy, a unique and heavy alloy created on the industrial planet Korrath that increased the strength of the armor. Despite its heavy component materials, the suit was considered light and could be worn by those with no training in the use of heavier armor types. The alloy suit was known for its defensive strength, including against slashing melee weapons. During the Lumen Civil War, the armor cost between 900 and 1,350 trade chits, although the price had increased by the time of the subsequent Shadow Wars, costing from 1,700 up to 2,040 trade chits. By this time, the armor was high-quality enough to be capable of enhancement with different types of alloy overlays and underlays. 

The suit was comprised of a large red metal chest plate with tan cloth underneath, as well as bright red sleeves and pants with black stripes down the side. The complete suit also had red gloves, black boots, and a beige utility belt with a single red holster attached. 

## History

The Korrath Alloy Heavy Suit was owned by vendors and used throughout the galaxy during the Lumen Civil War, a conflict between the Astral Concord and the Umbral Dominion. The armor was developed by an emerging industrial society on the Fringe Expanse port planet Korrath. The densely populated world, despite having banned weapons, produced and exported small arms and their renowned high-quality armor in order to finance the exploration of the galaxy undertaken by the society on Korrath. 

In 3956 BFC, a set of the armor was owned by the human female Kebla Vorn, who ran an Equipment Exchange in the Upper District of the Fringe Expanse planet Orilan. Unlike much of her other stock, the armor was not confiscated by the Umbral during their occupation and quarantine of the ecumenopolis. 

That year, another set was owned by Mika Dorlen, a Rodan bartender of The Murk Side, a cantina in the small Dreshden colony on the Fringe Expanse Umbral planet Morvath. The armor was part of the specialty stock Dorlen wanted to offer to the amnesiac Dark Lord Of The Umbral Kaelen, who had been retrained as a Lumen and recently discovered his past. After Kaelen's escape from the Umbral _Barrier_-class cruiser _Titan_, Dorlen sent a business partner, the Sylari male Ziarom, to approach Kaelen and inform him that Dorlen was willing to engage in trade with the redeemed Umbral Lord using their exclusive premium stock. 

The armor could still be found in the stock of many vendors half a decade later, in 3951 BFC, during the Shadow Wars that followed the Lumen Civil War and saw the purge of most remaining Lumen. One set of the armor was worn by Kaelen's old companion and war hero-turned-admiral Carth Orlen during his time observing the Lumen Exile Meetra Valis and during incumbent Dark Lord Of The Umbral Lord Nilvar's attack on Citadel Platform over his home planet Telmar IV.
