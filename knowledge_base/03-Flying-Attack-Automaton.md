# Flying Attack Automaton

"_Death comes to all, but these bargains are rare. Watch the automatons dance, friends, dazzle in their lethality!_ "
    ―Dracor Narthal 
**Flying Attack Automatons** were a type of floating, spherical automaton that the merchant Dracor Narthal sold from a stall on the asteroid Port Borga around 9 AFC. During a demonstration of the automatons, one poked an Aquan observer in the eye and another chased The renmor VX-5928. The automaton VX-8A49 later punched a flying attack automaton out of the air while defending VX-5928 and her friends. 

## Characteristics

    "_Fascinating weaponry. Absolutely novel toxicants._ "
    ―Dracor Narthal 
Flying Attack Automatons were a type of small, floating, spherical automaton with two limbs, one ending in a circular saw and the other in a trio of blades. The limbs were attached to a metallic gray band that ran around the circumference of the automatons' white bodies, and several red sensors were also located on the band. 

## History

    "_Our prices are lightyears from exorbitant and satisfaction is ensured! Buy now! Buy now! Before your enemy samples the famous bargains of Borgo._ "
    ―Dracor Narthal 
Around 9 AFC, the merchant Dracor Narthal sold flying attack automatons from a cart full of them on the pirate-infested asteroid Port Borga, demonstrating the automatons' abilities to those that passed by. The merchant called the automatons a bargain and advertised them as lethal assassins with fascinating weaponry and novel toxins. During one demonstration, Orial threw a piece of fruit up into the air for a automaton to slice in half. 

An Aquan observer then poked one of the demonstration automatons, which responded by poking the Aquan in the eye. One of the automatons then began pursuing the watching child VX-5928, who backed away from it into her friend Praronthal, after which the automaton stopped following her. When a group of pirates later surrounded VX-5928, Praronthal, and their friends Zengal and Narsar with the intent to rob them, the automaton VX-8A49 came to their defense. VX-8A49 punched the pirate Dornisar into a stand by Orial's attack automaton stall, attracting the attention of a trio of the flying attack automatons, one of whom flew at VX-8A49 with its saw active. VX-8A49 then punched the attack automaton out of the air.
