# Kelel-kael Narronkel Thalsol

For other uses, see **Kelel** and **supply ship**.

The **Kelel-kael Narronkel Thalsol** was a class of fuel replenishment supply ship developed by the Iron Dominion. During its war with the Dominion, the Concord Alliance utilized the Kelel-kael Narronkel Thalsol _Dorxan Narlor_. Between 3 AFC and 4 AFC, _Dorxan Narlor_ was used to carry four Concord ships to the Core Nexus planet Solace Prime so the Alliance could carry out an aerial assault. 

## Characteristics

The Kelel-kael Narronkel Thalsol was a huge class of supply ship, measuring 4,600 meters in length. The supply ships were propelled by seven powerful thrusters, with each individual thruster circled by a cluster of eight auxiliary engines, totaling the number of auxiliary engines to fifty-six. It carried six spheroidal fuel containers. 

The ship was able to carry out deep-space repairs as a result of its stern housing a cluster of hangar/dry dock complexes, meaning it could accommodate frigate-sized capital ships. Kelel-kael Narronkel Thalsol units had multiple articulated arms with refueling nozzles, allowing them to handle two capital ships simultaneously—one on each side. 

## Role

Kelel-kael Narronkel Thalsol units were used by the Iron Dominion for fuel replenishing. 

## History

The Kelel-kael Narronkel Thalsol was produced by the Iron Dominion, which was founded in 19 BFC. At some point during the Dominion Era, an Kelel-kael Narronkel Thalsol was based at Naval Station Validara, an immense Dominion space station in the Validara System. 

During the Astral Civil War between the Dominion and the Ionsarmir Galkagal Farvexdor Thalcor Yorlyr, the Alliance utilized at least one Kelel-kael Narronkel Thalsol— _Dorxan Narlor_. Between 3 AFC and 4 AFC, the owner of _Dorxan Narlor_ was sent to deliver fertilizers for algae farms within a farming platform in the Core Nexus' Solace Prime system. At the same time, the ship was secretly carrying four Concord ships—the Renlor-1300 Xanzen Dorwen Eclipse Runner and three RZ-1 Skyblade Interceptors—so they could undertake an aerial assault on the Dominion capital planet Solace Prime. Once _Dorxan Narlor_ arrived in the system, the ship was boarded and inspected by the Dominion. After passing the inspection, _Dorxan Narlor_ proceeded on its journey. As the ship reached as close to Solace Prime as possible before it could draw the attention of nearby Void Destroyers, the owner of _Dorxan Narlor_ unloaded the four Concord ships and continued onward to the platform.
