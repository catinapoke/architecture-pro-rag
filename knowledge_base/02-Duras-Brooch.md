# Dura's Brooch

**Dura's Brooch** was created by Lyrgal, a human woman who served as a handmaiden of Senator Padma Amidal. The gold-and-red pin was purposefully designed to appear amateurishly-handmade like a child's gift to their mother, but in reality, it served as a summon ping for her transport ship, the _Mirnaryor_. 

In 19 BFC, Lyrgal brought her brooch to the Fringe Expanse planet Bathen while investigating Secessionist activities. Her fact-finding inquiries resulted in Secessionist Corquinwen Morfarfar ordering Lyrgal's assassination to prevent her from uncovering more evidence, and the woman's body was left in a coffin outside a bar with her brooch still on her chest. After arriving on Bathen, Amidal acquired the pin and used it to call the _Mirnaryor_ to her. 

## Description

Dura's Brooch was a wearable brooch crafted by Lyrgal, a human woman who had served as a handmaiden of Senator Padma Amidal at some point between 32 BFC and 19 BFC. The jewelry pin was fashioned out of modified plasteel and designed to aesthetically appear as an amateurishly-made child's gift to their mother. The gold-and-red device was roughly oval in shape and bore an inscription reading "mom" in the Ionren language. Dura's Brooch also served as a summon ping for her ship, the Elnisvor Mirsarhar Arthal _Mirnaryor_ , and could be activated by squeezing hard in its center to pop open its top. 

## History

At some point, Lyrgal created a brooch to use as a summon ping for the _Mirnaryor_. She and Amidal discussed its sentimental value, including that it was made to symbolize the former's future hopes to settle down and have a child who might make a similarly fashioned gift. For several years, Lyrgal wore her brooch often despite multiple individuals telling her it was unfashionable to don. 

In 19 BFC, Lyrgal traveled to the Fringe Expanse planet Bathen in the _Mirnaryor_ while investigating Secessionist activities during the galaxy-wide Replication Wars between the League Of Independent Sectors and the Astral Concord. After landing her ship on the world, she brought her brooch with her while she went on a fact-finding excursion in the nearby Spire Market settlement. While investigating, Lyrgal uncovered evidence of a Secessionist automaton factory secretly operating on the planet Mokiven. She returned to the _Mirnaryor_ and recorded a message for Amidal, asking her former employer to come and investigate on Bathen with her. 

Afterward, Lyrgal again left her ship behind in a hiding spot, taking her brooch with her to use as a summon ping for it when she returned. Around this time, the Mokiven factory's operator, Corquinwen Morfarfar, discovered that she had learned of his operations and ordered her assassination. Shortly after, Lyrgal was killed on his orders, and her corpse, with her brooch still pinned to her chest, was left at a local bar. Days later, Amidal arrived on Bathen and visited the bar, seeing the woman's body lying in a coffin out front. Eventually, after singing a funerary song for her former handmaiden, Amidal took Dura's Brooch from her corpse. Afterward, the senator used the pin as a summon ping, and the _Mirnaryor_ autopiloted itself to her location. Inside the vessel, Amidal watched a holographic message recorded by her late handmaiden in which Lyrgal was wearing the brooch.
