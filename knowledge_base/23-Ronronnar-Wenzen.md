# Ronronnar Wenzen

**Ronronnar Wenzen** , also known simply as **Praion** , was a company active during the Lumen Civil War, fought between the Umbral Dominion and the Astral Concord. During that time, Praion produced high-end cybernetic implants. Their products included a type of implant known as the Veymon Package and a prototype device known as an auxiliary cortex. At the end of the Silent War, a period of unrest between the Concord and the reborn Umbral Dominion, the auxiliary cortex was one of many items stolen from the Dominion treasury starship _Kelsolfar Xankelren_ by a smuggler and two thieves named Ronthal Draprasar and Ronardra. 

## History

Ronronnar Wenzen, also known as Praion, was a company that was formed by the year 3956 BFC, during the Lumen Civil War, a conflict between the Umbral Dominion and the Astral Concord. By that time, Praion had produced the Veymon Package, a cybernetic implant that could fetch prices up to 7,500 trade chits on the open market. By the Silent War, a period of unrest between the Concord and the reborn Umbral Dominion, Ronronnar Wenzen had produced a prototype device known as an auxiliary cortex. Around the time that the Silent War escalated into the Astral War, the prototype was stored in one of the vaults of the Dominion treasury starship _Kelsolfar Xankelren_. However, the brain was one of many valuable items looted from the ship when it was raided by a smuggler and two thieves named Ronthal Draprasar and Ronardra. 

## Products

During its existence, Ronronnar Wenzen produced high-quality technological devices such as the prototype auxiliary cortex and the Veymon Package, which sold for around 7,500 trade chits around the time of the Lumen Civil War. The Veymon Package was a high-grade cybernetic implant that allowed organic beings equipped with it to have greater resistance to pain and stress.
