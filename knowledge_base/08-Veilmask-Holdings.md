# Veilmask Holdings

For other uses, see **Veilmask**.

**Veilmask Holdings** was a company that operated during the Concord Era. In 65 BFC, Veilmask Holdings and the Velara people entered a joint venture to establish a plasma refinery complex on the Mid Veil planet Velara. After the Fringe Expanse Construction And Assembly company completed its construction, Velara's Senator Shev Paltrine hosted an opening ceremony for the complex at some point after his election to the Astral Senate in 52 BFC. Before the start of the Replication Wars between the Astral Concord and the League Of Independent Sectors in 22 BFC, Veilmask Holdings also established a base on the volcanic and mineral-rich Fringe Expanse Territories planet Ashavar.

## Description

Veilmask Holdings was a company that carried out plasma refining operations. 

## History

### A joint venture

Veilmask Holdings operated during the Concord Era. At some point, evidence of a vast plasma source below the cliff face of the world's capital city, Theeden, was discovered. The Velara people of the Mid Veil planet Velara, who had been sourcing their power primarily from a series of outlying mines, opted to enter a joint venture with Veilmask Holdings to expand plasma refining on the planet. 

In 65 BFC, Veilmask Holdings—along with the company Fringe Expanse Construction And Assembly—helped initiate the construction of a plasma refinery complex on the Cliffs of Theeden, located strategically within the Theeden Royal Palace's Theeden Hangar. As the company's complex featured highly efficient generators, which provided more power than the city could use, the facility also maintained the capacity to export plasma produced in excess. Following the refinery complex's opening ceremonies, which occurred after Senator Shev Paltrine's election to the Astral Senate in 52 BFC, the establishment enriched Velara and became a popular tourist attraction. The ceremony was hosted by Paltrine, who was secretly an Umbral Lord with the title of Lord Malvek. 

### Continued operations

Though the factory was a joint venture between Veilmask Holdings and the Velara people, its day-to-day operations were managed by personnel from the Quinthalmo Harlor Corvexren unit of the Royal Velara Security Forces. However, the plasma refinery's continued operation posed a threat to the profits of another increasingly disgruntled company operating on the world: the Commerce League. These tensions eventually led the Commerce League—under Paltrine's heavy influence—to blockade Velara in 32 BFC. However, the blockade dissolved shortly afterward due to the defeat of the company's forces at the hands of the combined efforts of the Velara, the Grelon Grand Army, and their other allies. 

Prior to the start of the Replication Wars between the Astral Concord and League Of Independent Sectors in 22 BFC, Veilmask Holdings also established a base on the volcanic and mineral-rich Fringe Expanse Territories planet Ashavar. By around that time, they were one of several companies that wanted to exploit the world's mineral resources.
