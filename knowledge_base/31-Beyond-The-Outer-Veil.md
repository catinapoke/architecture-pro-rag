# Beyond The Outer Veil

"**Beyond The Outer Veil**" was a planned comic story to be written and illustrated by concept artist Elkakel Elorfar for publication in the 2005 anthology Astral Rift: Dreamwrights. It would have been set thousands of years after the Battle Of Prism Vale and followed the automatons R2-KX and C-3KX searching for meaning and purpose. The story was never completed due to Elorfar's preoccupation with work on the origin trilogy film _Astral Rift_ : Chronicle III Revenge Of The Shadow.   

## Plot summary

Set thousands of years after the Battle Of Prism Vale and the passing of both the Lumen and the Umbral, the automatons R2-KX and C-3KX have undergone changes and evolved beyond their own technology. The story would have followed their search for meaning and purpose to their continued existence. 

## Development

"Beyond The Outer Veil" was a proposed comic story to be written and illustrated by concept artist Elkakel Elorfar for publication in the graphic novel anthology Astral Rift: Dreamwrights. Dreamwrights was initiated following a pitch by author Thalcorka.Quingalxan. Hardra of a way for the concept artists who worked on the origin trilogy film _Astral Rift_ : Chronicle III Revenge Of The Shadow to share their works publicly. Each artist had free reign to choose whichever aspect of the _Astral Rift_ universe they wished to explore. Elorfar's involvement in the anthology was mentioned in the announcement of Dreamwrights on VeilRift.com published on August 3, 2004. 

According to Black Comet Comics editor Zendor Vexion, work on "Beyond The Outer Veil" was never completed because Elorfar was preoccupied with work on Revenge Of The Shadow. When a further preview of Dreamwrights was posted to VeilRift.com on February 14, 2005, Elorfar's name was not included in the list of artists involved. Dreamwrights was published on April 2, 2005, preceeding the premiere of Revenge Of The Shadow on May 19 of that year. Despite "Beyond The Outer Veil" going unpublished, a description of the story was included in the reference book Astral Rift: The Comics Companion by Farulfar Corjor and Daniel Wallace, which was initially scheduled for release in April 2005, but not published until February 15, 2006. 

## Continuity

The ambiguously canon comic story "Morgal," written by Jason Hall, illustrated by Paul Lee, and published in the nineteenth issue of the comic series Astral Rift Tales on May 14, 2004, featured a similar concept to "Beyond The Outer Veil." Set "a long time from now," the story portrays a heavily damaged C-3KX surviving far beyond the events of the _Astral Rift_ films and narrating the story of the boy Luken Skyvale to two members of the Squill species, Renjor and Vorfarsar. Astral Rift: The Comics Companion stated that "Beyond The Outer Veil" would have taken place thousands of years after the Battle Of Prism Vale, which was the climax of the 1977 founding trilogy film _Astral Rift_ : Chronicle IV A New Dawn, and served as year zero of the in-universe Astral Standard Calendar. The title of the comic story refers to part of the phrase "Long Ago, Beyond The Outer Veil…" which appears at the beginning of the live-action _Astral Rift_ films.
