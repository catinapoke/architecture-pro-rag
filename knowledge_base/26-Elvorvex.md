# Elvorvex

"_This one is definitely Brith, a recording method of an isolated island race, the Elvorvex, that concocted the knotting style as a means of recording genealogies —_'_strings of ancestors._ '__"
    ―Dr. Rotham

The **Elvorvex** were a subspecies of the Brith, a sentient species from Clakdor Prime. The Elvorvex lived on remote islands, isolated from other Brith communities. The Elvorvex developed a form of communication that involved tying knots of string to convey elaborate symbology. The Dark Lady Of The Umbral Vexara used a string of Elvorvex symbology as part of a collection of prophetic Umbral tassels that she used to attract Jace Solen, whom she later turned to the shadow aspect of Synth Flux. 

## Biology and appearance

The Elvorvex were one of the subspecies of the Brith—others included the Y'brith—a sentient species of highly advanced cranopods. 

## Society and culture

The Elvorvex developed a form of knot-tying in which they recorded their genealogy—literally "strings" of ancestors. This eventually evolved into a more complex form of communication, capable of conveying recorded thoughts and statements. 

## History

    "_He will ruin those who deny justice._ "
    ―An Elvorvex knot string

The Elvorvex lived on remote islands on the planet Clakdor Prime, located in the Maygar Sector of the Fringe Expanse Territories. As the result of a Brith civil war centuries prior to the Astral Civil War, their homeworld was transformed into a dangerous wasteland. The Elvorvex lived in isolation from the rest of the Brith species. 

Prior to the events of the Second Astral Civil War, the Dark Lady Of The Umbral Vexara used an Elvorvex knot string as part of a series of tassels containing prophecies about Jace Solen. The Elvorvex strand contained the message "He will ruin those who deny justice." Vexara left the series of tassels aboard Toryaz Hub in 40 AFC, trusting Solen would be attracted to the artifact, and in the process of discovering its meaning be led to her. Solen eventually obtained a translation of the Elvorvex symbology from Doctor Prathal Zenyor of Lorren. Knowing the tassels would bring Solen to Lorren, Vexara waited for him on the planet, and made contact with him shortly after his arrival, beginning a relationship that would eventually lead him to become the Umbral Lord Caedus.
