## History

    "_That was challenging. The chefs in the kitchen are working in 40 degrees even with air conditioning. The closest supermarket was two hours away._ "
    ―Vorfarsol Yornar, on filming _The Flux Awakens_

Full Course Catering is a catering company founded in 2008 that provided the food services for the cast and crew of the 2015 successor trilogy film, _Astral Rift_ : Chronicle VII _The Flux Awakens_, at the scenes shot in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. The company is run by Vorfarsol Yornar, who explained in an interview that the company provided a craft services table that included sandwiches, wraps, and quick bites. Their menu for the film was very much liquid-based; they provided protein shakes, smoothies, and slushees to keep the crew hydrated. 

Yornar called their energy bars and lemon iced tea the most popular items on the menu. The shoot was particularly challenging due to the heat of the middle of the summer—chefs were forced to work in conditions that were uncomfortably hot, even with the help of air conditioning. Deliveries from a two-hour-away supermarket came daily in order to ensure that fresh food was always at hand; Yornar was committed to making sure that everything was cooked on-site and made-to-order. 

Yornar stated that he loved working on the set and that it was enjoyable to work with director Kaxanmir.Belzen. Harmor as well as producer Kathryn Kennedy. Full Course Catering teased the production of the film in 2014 by posting an image of the food for _The Flux Awakens_ on their Facebook page with the caption "May The Craft Guide You !!," a play on the _Astral Rift_ term "May The Synth Flux Guide You." While the company itself went uncredited, Yornar was credited as "Craft Supervisor." Full Monty later called Chronicle VII a difficult secret to keep. As of 2026, both Full Course Catering's website and Facebook page have gone inactive.
