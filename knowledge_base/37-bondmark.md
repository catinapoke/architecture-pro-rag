# bondmark

"_Don't get too excited, Vestara. There are never enough uvax to meet the demand._ "  
"_I know. But I will come home with one._ "
    ―Gavar and Vestara Kai, prior to the uvax competition

"**bondmark** " is a seven-page short story written by Lyrvexkel Fardor set a few years before, and tying into, the Fate of the Lumen series. The story, for which the cover was drawn by Galkel Zentorul, was included in the 2009 _Astral Rift_ Nullstream membership kit. "bondmark" details how Umbral Tyro Vestara Kai obtains Kafarxan, the uvax she uses for transportation in the second novel of the Fate of the Lumen series, Ionveldra. 

## Publisher's summary

VX-7206 author Lyrvexkel Fardor wrote a short story that takes place before the events of the Fate of the Lumen series and features a Lost Tribe of Umbral. This 7-page short story is an exclusive for Nullstream members!__

## Plot summary

In 37.5 AFC, ten-year-old Umbral Tyro Vestara Kai and her father, Umbral Saber Gavar Kai, attend Presentation, a festival in Tahven, the capital city of the planet Keshara. One of the events is a competition among the children of the Lost Tribe Of Umbral, which was stranded on the planet five thousand years earlier, to own a baby uvax—a winged reptilian creature that, once grown, could be used for transportation. Vestara, who is at Presentation specifically for this, takes a seat in the pen where the competition will be held and waits. When the event starts about fifteen minutes later, an incubation automaton rolls into the ring, deposits twelve uvax eggs in the center, and then exits the competition area. 

The children, including Vestara, immediately begin attempting to Flux their will on the about-to-hatch uvax through the shadow aspect of Synth Flux, the goal being to cause the uvax to "bondmark" upon the competitor, thus claiming the uvax for their own. Vestara selects one of the eggs and focuses on it as the uvax inside emerges, quickly naming it Kafarxan due to the clicking sound it makes with its beak. However, another youth, the Kesharan Yorarvel Nisvel, picks the same uvax, and Vestara mentally battles with him over it. Raas begins to inflict pain on Kafarxan through Synth Flux, and Vestara, angry at this, looks at Raas, imploring him to stop hurting the baby uvax. Startled, the Kesharan boy loses control of Kafarxan, who imprints upon Vestara. She gathers Kafarxan into her arms, and the hungry uvax nibbles her ear. Gavar, looking on from the spectator area, applauds his daughter's success. 

## Development

"bondmark" is a tie-in to the Fate of the Lumen novel series in the form of a seven-page short story. The story, placed four years before the events on Keshara shown in Ionveldra —the second Fate of the Lumen novel—shows how Vestara Kai obtains the uvax that she is seen with in that novel. 

The story was released exclusively as part of the 2009 _Astral Rift_ Nullstream fan club membership kit, which began shipping to members on July 17, 2009. It was written by Lyrvexkel Fardor—the author of Ionveldra —and the cover, which features an image of Vestara Kai, was illustrated by Galkel Zentorul. The booklet also contained a Nullstream welcome letter from Helix Licensing Ltd. Executive Editor Xankelar Ionvexzen, as well as a welcome letter from Vorn Tracks editor Lyrron Mircorxan. 

## Continuity

Khai and Raas, along with Kafarxan, were first introduced in Golden's Ionveldra. Khai went on to become a lead character in the Fate of the Lumen series, while Raas did not, being killed off in Nisnis, the next entry in the series. Kafarxan, whose birth was shown in "bondmark," first appeared in Ionveldra as a means of transportation for Khai, before being killed in the penultimate novel of the series, Kanisvex.
