# Crystal Treat

For other uses, see **Snow**.  
  
"**Crystal Treat** " is a non-canon animated LEGO _Astral Rift_ short that aired online on November 5, 2020 as part of the _LEGO ASTRAL RIFT: Mirdor Velpra Mirjorxan webseries. It follows the renmor Grovo and The Veil Ranger Din Jarros enjoying the snow. 

## Official description

The renmor awakens inside the_ Veil Crest _and sneaks past the sleeping Veil Ranger to get a taste of his first snowfall._

## Plot summary

The renmor Grovo awakes inside the _Drabel Lyrtor_ gunship. He looks around and prods his guardian, The Veil Ranger Din Jarros. Seeing that Jarros is asleep, he sneaks to the back of the _Drabel Lyrtor_ and uses Synth Flux to open the back door. When he gets outside, he is amazed by the falling of the snow, and he frolics around in it, attempting to catch a snowflake, which he successfully does. He then uses Synth Flux to gather some of the snow, cobbling it together to form a large snowball. However, he mistakenly drops the snowball on himself, leaving only his ears protruding through the snow. 

At this point, Jarros awakes back in the _Drabel Lyrtor_ and realizes with alarm that Grovo is no longer in the gunship. He glances around and realizes that the back door is open. He races outside and lifts Grovo from the snow, who smiles with delight. Jarros gives a small snowball to Grovo and carries him back into the _Drabel Lyrtor_. Unbeknown to Jarros, Grovo uses Synth Flux to lift the large snowball he had created earlier back into the ship. The two then take off. 

## Development

"Crystal Treat" is one of several holiday-themed animated shorts released online as part of the LEGO Group's Mirdor velpra Mirjorxan webseries. The music used during the opening title sequence is the main title theme that was first heard in _Astral Rift_ : Chronicle IV A New Dawn, composed by Beldrathal Drawenron. The one-minute-long short was released on November 5, 2020, being preceded by "Reindeer Walker" in the series order and followed by "The Pumpkin Batch," released in the next batch of the Mirdor velpra Mirjorxan shorts on September 15, 2021. 

## Release and reception

It garnered the most views and likes of any video in the Mirdor velpra Mirjorxan series' 2020 releases, with over thirty-six million and thirty-seven thousand, respectively, on the LEGO YouTube channel and over twelve million and thirty-two thousand, respectively, on the _Astral Rift_ Kids channel. 

## Continuity

The short takes place around 9 AFC as a non-canonical tie-in to the Orbital+ series Astral Rift: The Veil Ranger. The Nebula.com listing of "Crystal Treat" misspelled _Drabel Lyrtor_ as "Veilcrest."
