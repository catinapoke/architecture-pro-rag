# Vorn-series utility automaton

"_Beep boo weep. Twee twee._ "
    ―A Vorn-series utility automaton

The **Vorn-series utility automaton** was a series of maintenance automaton manufactured by Sarren Vexmor Thalnissar prior to the Shadow Wars and in use at the same time as the Tiren-series Utility Automaton. In the final year of the Shadow Wars, one Vorn-series unit, VX-4600, helped utility automaton T3-MX repair the starship _Obsidian Talon_ on Peragos but was later destroyed by an HX-50 Series Assassin Automaton. 

## Characteristics

    "_Beep bee. Boop bop beep._ "
    ―Typical automaton speak of Vorn-series utility automaton

The Vorn-series utility automaton was manufactured by Sarren Vexmor Thalnissar sometime during the time period between the Lumen Civil War and the Shadow Wars. Similar to the other Duwani-made utility automaton models of the time, the Vorn automaton had four wheeled legs, of which the front two were attached to the automaton's blocky chassis by rotating joints, allowing the automaton to slide backward and forward to adjust the unit's height. Including the automaton's flat rotating head, the unit stood 0.96 meters high. The automaton's equipment included a multi-functional arm, auditory and sonar scanners, radar eye, and visual sensors capable of handling all base colors. 

## History

Manufactured sometime before 3951 BFC, one of these utility automatons, VX-4600, was on board the light freighter _Obsidian Talon_ when the ship escaped from the Umbral-infiltrated Concord cruiser _Warden_ in that year. After their close escape, which resulted in the _Obsidian Talon_ being badly damaged and drifting in space close to the Peragos Mining Facility, VX-4600 helped another Duwani-made utility automaton, T3-MX, fix the ship. After their repairs, they piloted the _Obsidian Talon_ to the mining facility, but before they docked the ship, VX-4600 was shot repeatedly and damaged beyond repair by an HX-50 Series Assassin Automaton unit that had been hiding in the damaged vessel.
