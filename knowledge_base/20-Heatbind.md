# Heatbind

**Heatbind** was a Flux Technique that allowed its user to draw on the power of Synth Flux to remain warm in a cold environment. The Lumen Knight Jace Solen once used this Flux Technique, employing it in conjunction with a Flux Shield during the Battle Of Ebar in 28 AFC to survive in a mine that had lost its atmosphere. 

## Description

The art of keeping warm in a cold environment, Heatbind required the user to concentrate on Synth Flux to retain their body heat. 

## Applications

Utilizing Heatbind to retain one's body heat was used by Lumen in hostile environments, such as vacuum. If a Flux-sensitive knowledgeable in Heatbind found themselves in a cold environment, they could draw on Synth Flux to keep themselves warm. 

## Users

Heatbind was an ability once displayed by Lumen Knight Jace Solen, who used the technique during the Battle Of Ebar of the Yuzhan Tide War in 28 AFC. When his mentor, Vergara, crashed an RZ-1 Skyblade Interceptor into the Ebar 9 moon, perishing on impact and causing the mines in which Solen was fighting to lose atmosphere, he was forced to use Heatbind in conjunction with a Flux Shield to survive in the depressurized area. He found it difficult to concentrate on Heatbind and on maintaining the Flux Shield that retained his air, and so he began to shiver. He managed to hold on with the help of Vergara's spirit before help arrived.
