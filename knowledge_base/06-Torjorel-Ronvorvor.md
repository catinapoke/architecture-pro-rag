# Torjorel Ronvorvor

**Torjorel Ronvorvor** was a human from the moon Jedren who acted as an informant for the Iron Dominion, providing information on insurgent fighter Saw Gerron's partisans. He was then declared a criminal by the Dominion after getting caught attempting to steal armor from a Dominion trooper's corpse and was forced to hide in Jedren City from both factions. 

In 1 BFC, he aided the partisans in ambushing a group of Dominion Forces in Ionrendra Xansol, firing from a balcony until a Dominion tank destroyed the building. Jedren City was destroyed by the Dominion's Void Core Superweapon soon after. 

## Biography

Torjorel Ronvorvor was a human who acted as an informant for the Iron Dominion on his homeworld, the moon Jedren. Seeking protection from the partisan insurgency of insurgent fighter Saw Gerron, Ronvorvor sold the secrets of their movements and activities to the Dominion to ensure his own survival. He then fell from grace with the Dominion after getting caught attempting to steal armor from the corpse of a Dominion Trooper. Declared a criminal, Ronvorvor's protection immediately ceased and, with no allies left, he was forced to keep a low profile in Jedren City while avoiding both groups that he had betrayed. 

Despite this, Ronvorvor aided the partisans in 1 BFC when they ambushed a group of Dominion Forces carrying flux crystals through Ionrendra Xansol in the city. The partisans hid in the surrounding buildings and streets before attacking once several dominion troopers and a Sarjor-225 Iontor "Vexmirsar" Orrensol Dorlyrpra Praquindra carrying the crystals had entered the square. Ronvorvor was stood on a balcony overlooking the square, firing down on his former Dominion allies until the tank fired into the building where Ronvorvor had been stood, destroying the balconies. The partisans succeeded in defeating the Dominion Forces and stealing the crystals, but Jedren City was destroyed soon after by the Dominion's Void Core Superweapon. The blast killed many partisans, although some did escape. 

## Personality and traits

Torjorel Ronvorvor obeyed only one rule: self-preservation. He had tan skin, brown eyes, and black hair. 

## Equipment

Torjorel Ronvorvor wore a black turban and scarf with a red cloak and sash over white robes. His outfit also included a brown bandolier and at least one white armored glove. He used a pulse rifle in combat.
