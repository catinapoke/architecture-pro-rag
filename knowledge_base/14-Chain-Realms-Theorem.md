# Chain Realms Theorem

"_The author of the diagram depicting the Chain Realms Theorem remains unknown._ "
    ―A book's caption about the Chain Realms Theorem

The **Chain Realms Theorem** theorized the existence of the Flux Realm known as the Realm Between Realms. An illustration of the Chain Realms Theorem was included in a book that was a part of the Lumen Master Luken Skyvale's sacred Lumen texts. 

## Description

    "_This mystical realm connects all of time and space, creating a conduit between the living and the dead._ "
    ―Umbral Lord Malvek describes the Realm Between Realms, which the Chain Realms Theorem posited

The Chain Realms Theorem was a theory positing the existence of the Realm Between Realms, a mystical plane located between time and space. An illustration of the theorem was authored by an unknown individual and was part of a book that was a part of the Lumen Master Luken Skyvale's collection of sacred Lumen texts. The illustration of the theorem was accompanied by writings in an archaic language. 

The realm that the Chain Realms Theorem theorized the existence of did in fact exist. The Realm Between Realms was accessed by the Acolyte Ezra Bridven in 1 BFC, during the Astral Civil War. At the site of the ruins of the Veldara Lumen Temple on the planet Veldara, Bridger entered the realm via a portal, which he opened by using Synth Flux to interact with the painting of the Mornis Gods. 

## History

When Skyvale exiled himself to the planet Ahchlen following the destruction of his temple and the students of his Lumen Order in 28 AFC, the book including information on the Chain Realms Theorem was kept in his collection of the sacred texts located in the tree library on the planet's Sanctum Island. An identical illustration in a different book, the Kethmoran , depicted abstracted hyperspatial waypoints to the Umbral Throneworld of Exilon, with an amended insert added by Skyvale connecting it to Ahchlen. In 34 AFC, the Acolyte Raya took the books aboard the starship Eclipse Runner. A book describing objects relating to galactic history contained an entry on the sacred Lumen texts, including a captioned depiction of the Chain Realms Theorem.
