# Painward

"_Through Synth Flux, a Lumen can endure even the most debilitating pain._ "
    ―Count Dural, to Farel Tordra Velron 
**Painward** was a Lumen Flux Power used to endure physical pain. During the Battle Of Nar Shaden, Lumen Master Thalcorron used the technique to mitigate the pain of being immolated. Lumen Master Renor Jornis taught the technique to the Lumen Count Dural at some point between 102 BFC and 68 BFC, and he later passed on this knowledge to his two Acolytes: first Yorhar Lorwenfar, and later Farel Tordra Velron. 

## Description

Painward was a Flux Power used by several Lumen to endure physical pain. Masters of the ability could resist otherwise-deadly injuries and pain. It was also possible to use Painward to make others resist pain. 

## History

    "_This technique is not one I learned from Master Zephara. And yet I will share it with you, as I shared it with Rael. Who knows? Perhaps you will share it with your Acolyte one day._ "
    ―Count Dural to Farel Tordra Velron about Painward

Lumen Master Thalcorron endured the pain of being immolated during the Battle Of Nar Shaden between the Lumen Order and the Ember Bearers through Synth Flux. At some point between 102 BFC and 68 BFC, Lumen Master Renor Jornis taught the technique to fellow Lumen Count Dural. Count Dural taught it to his two Acolytes: Yorhar Lorwenfar, whom he trained from around 80 BFC to around 70 BFC, and, after taking him on in 68 BFC, Farel Tordra Velron. To show the technique to Jinn, Count Dural placed a metal band that the Pranar used as a torture device on his own chest. 

Count Dural later told his sister, Arzen, about his conversation with Jinn in a holo-letter. Around 23 BFC, Count Dural gave several data disks containing his messages to Arzen to his recently recruited Umbral Assassin—Asajj Ventra—for her investigation into his sister's location, and she listened to the holo-disk that mentioned Painward.
