# Suudaa Nemro's disease

"_My friend's old employer is a Magnate Cartel boss named Nemro. He's infected with an incurable disease. It's eating him alive —literally._"  
    ―Praquin

During the Silent War between the Astral Concord and the reconstituted Umbral Dominion, the Magnate Suudaa Nemro became infected with a **disease** that slowly dissolved his biological tissue. Nemro was cured by the xenopathologist Harvel Kelron, who had studied the disease while in medical school and was able to develop a treatment using the chemicals ardavium, accelerite, and cyanate silicate. However, the Houkan bounty hunter Nisthalion killed Nemro before reporters from the Kalyrvor Ulmirar Yormor could verify Praquin's achievement. 

## Description

    "_The treatment formula's ready, and good thing —Nemro's half the Magnate he used to be._"
    ―Praquin

A disease gradually dissolved the victim's biological tissue over time, and the Magnate species was susceptible to infection by it. While it was considered incurable by the time of the Silent War between the Astral Concord and the Umbral Dominion, the xenopathologist Harvel Kelron, better known as Praquin, was able to formulate an experimental cure for the disease using the primary ingredients of cyanate silicate, ardavium, and accelerite. 

## History

    "_No one's ever cured what Nemro has. He'll pay top trade chits to any doctor who can save his life. This is my big chance._ "
    ―Praquin

Around 3640 BFC, the Magnate Suudaa Nemro was infected with the disease on the planet Nal Marava. A powerful crime lord, Nemro offered a significant sum of trade chits to any doctor who could save his life. While traveling with the Lumen Knight known as the Hero Of Tythos, Praquin was alerted to Nemro's affliction by a former acquaintance named Jorbel Morvorvel, a Rattaki who had formerly worked as Nemro's enforcer. Remembering that he had studied the disease during his time attending the First Concord Medical Academy Of Solace Prime, Praquin began to work on an experimental cure that had been proposed but never utilized in an effort to develop a cure and thereby gain himself both Nemro's trade chits and galactic fame. 

After successfully developing a cure, Praquin offered his treatment to Nemro, who had lost almost half of his biological mass by that time. However, in order to ensure that Nemro did not try and compel him into the Magnate's employment, Praquin arranged for Djannis to pose as a hostage on the Hero Of Tythos's _Defender_ -class light corvette, as Nemro had a soft spot for his former enforcer. Praquin traveled to Nemro's Estate on Nal Marava and successfully cured Nemro of his disease, halting the Magnate's dissolution and earning Nemro's gratitude. However, Praquin's attempt to gain fame by having his achievement recorded in the Kalyrvor Ulmirar Yormor failed when Nemro was killed by a Houkan bounty hunter named Nisthalion, whom Nemro had crossed several years earlier. The Journal's reporters were unable to verify Praquin's treatment, as Nisthalion's assault had left very little of Nemro's body behind.
