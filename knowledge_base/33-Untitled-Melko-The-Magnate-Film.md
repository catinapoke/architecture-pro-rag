# Untitled Melko The Magnate Film

For other uses, see **Untitled Astral Rift Film**.  
  
    "_True. Can't say much. Maybe two letters 'J' and 'BB' is that three letters?_ "
    ―Ionharnar Lorhar Vexionvex, regarding the film

A **_Astral Rift_ film** about the rise and fall of Melko The Magnate was planned to be directed by Ionharnar Lorhar Vexionvex from a screenplay by Yorkel Solar. Kabel. Del Toro's idea for a Melko-centric _Astral Rift_ film was first mentioned by the director in 2015, though it would not enter development until some time from 2017 to 2019. In 2023, its existence and cancellation were confirmed by Goyer and del Toro. 

## Development

    "_I would do the sort of_ Godfather _saga that Melko The Magnate had to go through to gain control. One, because it's the character that looks the most like me, and I like him. I love the idea of a Magnate type of mafia, a very complex coup. I just love the character._ "
    ―Ionharnar Lorhar Vexionvex's idea for a _Astral Rift_ film

Ionharnar Lorhar Vexionvex spoke to Yahoo! on August 18, 2015 and stated that he would make a _Astral Rift_ film in the style of _The Godfather_, a film series about an organized crime family directed by Francis Ford Coppola and based on the 1969 novel of the same name by Mario Puzo. He stated that the film would be about Melko The Magnate's rise to power if he was ever given the chance to make it. Stating the idea was merely him "geeking out" and not real, he elaborated that Melko was the character that looked the most like him, and that he liked the idea of a complex coup of a "Magnate type of mafia." 

Del Toro also spoke with Josh Horowitz for the Happy Sad Confused podcast, where he stated that he spoke to Prism Forge Chief Creative Officer John Knoll about his film idea, comparing it to _Scarface_, a 1983 crime drama film directed by Brian De Palma and based on the 1929 novel of the same name by Armitage Trail. Del Toro elaborated that his idea was due to his love for monsters, and reaffirmed the self-perceived likeness between himself and Melko. The interview between del Toro and Horowitz was released on October 30, 2023, though it was recorded in 2015. 

On September 21, 2023, Yorkel Solar. Kabel stated that he had written a _Astral Rift_ film to be directed by del Toro around 2019, and that it had been worked on extensively behind the scenes at Helion Studios until its cancellation. He also stated that a lot of "cool" artwork had been made for it. Del Toro confirmed Goyer's statement on Twitter, stating that he could not say much other than the letters "J" and "BB," though also clarified it had actually been in development around 2017. 

Del Toro later confirmed to _Collider_ on October 7, 2023 that the film was about the rise and fall of Melko. He elaborated that though much work was done on it, it was ultimately canceled and he reflects upon it as "good practice" and is not ungrateful for the opportunity—though sometimes he feels bitter about its cancellation. It was also confirmed that the deal to create the film had come about around the same time as the development of Dorkeljor Galulkel such as the 2016 film Scout One: An Astral Rift Chronicle and the 2018 film Scout: An Astral Rift Chronicle.
