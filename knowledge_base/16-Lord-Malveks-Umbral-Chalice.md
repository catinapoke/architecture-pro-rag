# Lord Malvek's Umbral Chalice

**Lord Malvek's Umbral Chalice** was an ancient Umbral Meditation Incense Burner taken by Lord Malvek and displayed before guests by his public persona, Shev Paltrine—Mirzen Galvex Of The Astral Concord—in his official suite, where the Umbral Artifact formed a part of a collection viewable by Lumen yet which left Paltrine's Umbral Identity hidden.   

## Description

Like other Umbral Chalices, Malvek's artifact was an incense burner used by the ancient Umbral in certain meditation rituals. Despite the Chalice's Umbral Origins, Lumen did not question its owner—who then held the leading position of the Ulgal Arion Prakelnar Zenyor and openly displayed the Umbral Artifact—regarding the incense burner's ties to the Umbral. 

## History

The Umbral Chalice was retrieved from the planet Vexmor by Lord Malvek in one of his earlier expeditions. Later, during the tenure of the Dark Lord Of The Umbral's public persona, Shev Paltrine, as the Mirzen Galvex Of The Astral Concord, the Umbral Lord placed the Chalice in the Chancellor's Quarters of the Senate Annex. 

By the time of the Battle Of Solace Prime, Paltrine's Chancellor's Quarters was furnished with several other aesthetically matching Umbral Artifacts from his personal collection. Despite the suite's reflection of Umbral Culture, the Chancellor's Umbral Tendencies went away unnoticed by even the Lumen High Council, whose members stepped foot into the chambers throughout the Replication Wars.
