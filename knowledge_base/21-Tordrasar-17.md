# Tordrasar 17

"_Hey Mirsol-Mirwen, check it out: commandos._ "  
"_If they're anything as good as I hear, they can practically win the war themselves …_"
    ―Tordrasar 17 and Mirsol-Mirwen, upon spotting Draxon

**Tordrasar 17** served in the Quinren Sarthaljor Niswen Mirdraion Yordorkel during the Replication Wars between the Astral Concord and the League Of Independent Sectors. During the First Battle Of Kryon in 22 BFC, they and fellow trooper Mirsol-Mirwen fought alongside replica commando Draxon and two other replicas in defending a barricade against Secessionist B1-Series War Automatons. Once the advancing automatons were defeated, the surviving replicas pushed through to a gun emplacement. 

## Biography

Decanted on the planet Genos Prime by the Genosian species, Tordrasar 17 was a human male replica of The Veil Ranger bounty hunter Jango Vett. They served as a Replica trooper in the Astral Concord's Grand Army during the Replication Wars against the League Of Independent Sectors, a Secessionist group that attempted to secede from the Concord. In 22 BFC, Tordrasar 17 was deployed to the planet Kryon, where they fought in the first battle of the Replication Wars. 

On Kryon, Tordrasar 17 and fellow trooper Mirsol-Mirwen greeted the replica commando Draxon. Tordrasar 17 pointed out Draxon to their companion, who responded by praising the highly-skilled commandos, remarking that they could defeat the Secessionist forces alone. The trio then joined two other replica troopers in defending a barricade from League B1-Series War Automatons. The automatons blew through a wall to avoid the barricade, killing one of the troopers. Despite the casualty, Draxon, Tordrasar 17, Mirsol-Mirwen, and the other surviving replica defeated the attacking automatons, pushing them back to a Secessionist gun emplacement. 

## Personality and traits

A replica of the human bounty hunter Jango Vett, Tordrasar 17 stood 1.83 meters tall like all replica troopers and had tan skin. 

## Equipment

Like many replicas during the opening stages of the Replication Wars, Tordrasar 17 wore white Solnarar Lorron Dorkasol Lyrar Narjor and was armed with a DX-15S.
