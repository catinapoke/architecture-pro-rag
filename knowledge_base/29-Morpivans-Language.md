# Morpivan's Language

"_We appreciate the time you've given us. Is there anything else you'd like to say?_ "  
"_Elhh-pochrr-fffff-snappp._ "  
"_Thank you._ "  
"_Llllnnnnnng._ "  
    ―A Astral Gossip interviewer and the Morpivan

A **language** was used by the being known as the Morpivan in an interview published in The Astral Gossip. The phrases of the language, when transcribed into the High Astral Alphabet, made heavy use of consecutively repeated consonants and vowels. The interview did not translate the Morpivan's replies. 
    * 1.1 Phrases

## Description

A language used by the alien being known as the Morpivan was once transcribed using the High Astral Alphabet. In that representation, the Morpivan's statements consisted of strings of letters that frequently used consecutively repeated consonants and vowels. The strings were separated from each other by dashes. 

### Phrases

  * _Elhh-pochrr-fffff-snappp._ — Given in response to "We appreciate the time you've given us. Is there anything else you'd like to say?"
  * _Fraw-fruh-fing-fng._ — Given in response to "Do you plan to mutate?"
  * _Leeeem-leeeeem-leeeeemm._ — Given in response to "Is it true that you practice prismatic self-defense?"
  * _Llllnnnnnng._ — Given in response to "Thank you."
  * _Nsssss-prrrripipp-poom._ — Given in response to "How many girlfriends have you had?"
  * _Pfsspn-blttt-nooooop-fzzzzz._ — Given in response to "What is your favorite food?"
  * _Smmmmmmmm-reeeem-perpppp._ — Given in response to "How old are you?"

## History

In an interview published in the The Astral Gossip publication, the Morpivan responded to the interviewer's questions, which were posed in the Meridian Basic Standard, in another language. An excerpt from the interview provided the Morpivan's comments in untranslated form, and the interview itself involved questions related to the Morpivan's favorite food, their age, the number of girlfriends they had had, and the Morpivan's practice of prismatic self-defense and future plans for mutating.
