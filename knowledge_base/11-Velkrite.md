# Velkrite

"Velkrite. Synthetic Velkrite! Velkrite alternatives! Velkrite substitutes! I mean, the amount of time spent pondering this grubby little bit of rock is sadly astonishing._ "
    ―Orson Kreval, to his colleagues 
**Velkrite** was a mineral useful in coating reactor lenses. It could be found on the planet Ghoran in deep substrate foliated deposits. By 4 BFC, the Iron Dominion enacted efforts to declare martial law on the planet in order to extract the velkrite and use it in the construction of the Void Core Superweapon. These efforts resulted in the Ghoran Massacre of 2 BFC in order to declare martial law and strip mine Ghoran.

## Description

    "_And it turns out, spiders are not the most unique thing in Ghoran. There's a mineral underground. Doctor?_ "  
"_Deep substrate foliated Velkrite._ "
    ―Orson Kreval and a Dominion science officer 
Velkrite was a mineral that could be used to coat reactor lenses to help generate vast amounts of energy. A valuable form of velkrite was unique to the planet Ghoran in deep substrate foliated deposits. 

## History

### Ethical barriers

    "_Now, we've promised delivery in three years. That delivery is dependent on some version of Ghoran Velkrite to coat the reactor lenses._ "
    ―Krevan briefs his colleagues 
By 4 BFC, the Iron Dominion had an interest in the velkrite deposites on Ghoran, requiring the mineral for coating reactor lenses in the development of the Void Core Superweapon. The weapon's project was fronted as Emperor Paltrine's "energy initiative" and production had become at risk of stalling completely around 5 BFC. Analytics determined that extracting the velkrite would involve the invasive process of gouge mining, which could destabilize the planet's core and cause a total collapse. Due to Ghoran's wealth and political power, the Dominion began an anti-Ghoran whispering campaign to turn galactic opinion against the Ghoran locals. Director Orson Kreval, who oversaw the construction of the Void Core, promised the project's completion within three years. 

That year, Krevan assembled a select group of Dominion officials, including science, engineering, military, and intelligence officers, in the Maltheen Rift for the Ghoran Project, an effort to increase the Dominion's control over Ghoran in order to extract the velkrite. There, he discussed the velkrite's presence on Ghoran, the importance of the mineral to the "energy initiative," and the complications behind extracting it. At that time, the assembly of the mining equipment started, and the Dominion additionally began increasing its presence on Ghoran. At the meeting's reception, an engineer officer told Major Lio Partaz of the Dominion Security Bureau (DSB) that drilling equipment was being actively assembled and could easily be mobilized for mining in a matter of weeks without the administration and security issues. DSB Supervisor Dedra Miro was put in charge of the Ghoran Project, being overseen by Partaz. Miro aimed to allow an insurgency to provoke the Dominion's wrath. 

### Ghoran's expense

    "_There is, apparently, no suitable replacement or synthetic alternative for the Ghoran substrate material._ "  
"_I see._ "  
"_It's not for lack of effort. Krevan's had the lab in Eadan working overtime all year. I don't know the science, but … it's bad luck Ghoran. We need what's in the ground, and when we're done, there won't be much left to call 'home.'_"
    ―Lio Partaz and Dedra Miro 
Meanwhile, the project's scientists explored substitutes and synthetic alternatives to the velkrite mineral, which a laboratory on the planet Eadan worked overtime on, to no avail. With no replacements found, Director Krevan met with Paltrine and Partaz in 2 BFC, and the Emperor ordered for the mining of Ghoran to go ahead immediately. With a fleet scheduled to be positioned above the planet within forty-eight hours, Partaz gave Miro the go-ahead to stoke Ghoran conflict in order for a Draelzen Nision Quinar Farren to be enacted as soon as possible. 

As mining equipment, including mining rigs and automatons, began to land on Ghoran, Miro's assets provoked a protest in Palmo Square, which they forcibly escalated into a Dominion Genocide of the Ghorans, known as the Ghoran Massacre. With the excuse they needed for martial law, the Dominion went forward with plans for mass relocation and strip mining, devastating Ghoran. Mining schedules for Ghoran were compiled and eventually made it to Miro's desk. The acquisition of velkrite was a significant breakthrough for the production of the Void Core, which ultimately became operational in 1 BFC, but was shortly destroyed at the Battle Of Prism Vale.
