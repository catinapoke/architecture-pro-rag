# Zendrathal95 Farlorsar

The **Zendrathal95 Farlorsar** was a small, concealable pulse weapon produced by Czeran Arms. The weapon was shaped like a savaq card with a long, thin needle, from which the pulse bolts were discharged. Zendrathal95 Farlorsars were used at formal events by assassins during the height of the Replication Wars. 

## Description

The Zendrathal95 Farlorsar was an incredibly small, lightweight pulse weapon shaped like a savaq card, which could be hexagonal or rectangular in shape. It was exotic and easy to conceal but had an effective range of only around an arm's length. 

Rather than using a Gavlen-pattern barrel like most pulse weapons, pulse weapon energy was channeled along and discharged from a long, thin needle that protruded from between the user's fingers. The weapon possessed a stun setting. 

## History

Czeran Arms designed and produced the Zendrathal95 Farlorsar, which cost 400 trade chits and competed in the niche micro-pulse weapon market. However, its reimagined design differentiated it from micro-pulse weapons, which generally conformed to the basic idea of a pulse weapon. Assassins made use of Zendrathal95 Farlorsars during the height of the Replication Wars, using them to target Astral Concord or League Of Independent Sectors senators at formal events. The weapon was also popular with grifters and con artists, as well as private security personnel, the latter of whom preferred the reduced risk of collateral damage caused by the Zendrathal95 Farlorsar's short range.
