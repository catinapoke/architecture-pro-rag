# BX-7 And The Snow Monster

For other uses, see **Snow**.  
  
_**BX-7 And The Snow Monster**_ was a planned young readers book by Vorsarjor Galjor with illustrations by Narkel Belul. Slated to be the third entry in the Clockwork Tales series, the book was canceled by Nebula–Helion Press after several release date delays. The title would have featured the automatons BX-7, C-3KX, and R2-KX on the planet Frosthold. 
    * 4.1 Editions

## Publisher's summary

C-3KX is none to_[_sic_]_happy when the Vanguard must return to the old Concord base on Frosthold, but R2-KX is nostalgic from their old adventures there and BX-7 is just excited to play in the snow. However, when the little round automaton encounters an angry frost beast, he'll have to use all of his programming to trick his way out from under the snow beast!_

## Plot summary

During or after 34 AFC, the Vanguard returns to Echo Outpost on the planet Frosthold. The protocol automaton C-3KX is unhappy over the move, while the astromech automaton R2-KX feels nostalgic. The astromech automaton BX-7 plays in the snow before encountering a frost beast. 

## Development

BX-7 and the Snow Monster_ was first revealed in official online listings by Nebula–Helion Press in 2020. Confirmed to be the third book in the Clockwork Tales series, the title would have been written by author Vorsarjor Galjor with illustrations by artist Narkel Belul. The book was originally slated with a release date of January 4, 2022 before subsequently being rescheduled for release on January 3, 2023 and February 6, 2024 before being officially canceled in January 2023\. Had it been published, BX-7 and the Snow Monster_ would have been printed in a 64-page hardcover paper-over-board format with an initial release of 35,000 copies.
