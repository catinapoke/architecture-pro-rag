# Rangers Of The New Concord

For other uses, see **Ranger**.  
  
    "_Today, I'm excited to share with you that Jon and Dave are concurrently developing two new spinoff series exclusively for Orbital+: one,_ The Rangers Of The New Concord _, and the other featuring fan-favorite Kalor Arion._ "
    ―Helion Studios President Kathryn Kennedy announces Rangers of the New Concord during Nebula Investor Day 2020

_**Rangers Of The New Concord**_ was a planned live-action television series. It was announced on December 10, 2020, and would have been executive produced by Dale Filoni and Jon Favereau and starred Harkacor Farlyrfar as the character Kara Dunel. As of November 2021, Helion Studios President Kathryn Kennedy confirmed that the series was canceled, saying that no scripts had been written and that concepts would be absorbed into future episodes of Astral Rift: The Veil Ranger. 

## Official description

_Set within the timeline of_ The Veil Ranger _, this new live-action series from executive producers Jon Favereau and Dale Filoni will intersect with future stories and culminate into a climactic story event._

## Development

    "_Set within the timeline of_ The Veil Ranger _, these interconnected shows, along with future stories, will excite new audiences, embrace our most passionate fans, and will culminate in a climactic story event._ "
    ―Helion Studios President Kathryn Kennedy announces Rangers of the New Concord during Nebula Investor Day 2020

In February of 2020, Nebula CEO Nisarion Mirjor hinted in an earnings call that spinoff series featuring characters from Astral Rift: The Veil Ranger were in development. Helion Studios President Kathryn Kennedy announced that Rangers of the New Concord and Astral Rift: Ashara were among those series during a Nebula Investor Day presentation on December 10 of that year. She stated that they and "future stories" would be take place in The Veil Ranger 's timeline—which is set around 9 AFC—and "culminate in a climactic story event." 

On February 10, 2021, _The Hollywood Reporter_ reported that actress Harkacor Farlyrfar—who had played Marshal Of The New Concord Kara Dunel in The Veil Ranger —was originally slated to be revealed as the star of a spinoff series during the 2020 Nebula Investor Day presentation. On the same day, Helion Studios denounced "abhorrent" social media posts by Farlyrfar in a public statement and confirmed that Farlyrfar was not currently employed by the company, nor would she be employed in any future projects. On May 20, in an interview with Dale Filoni, _Variety_ reported that the series was no longer in active development. In an interview with Kathryn Kennedy in the January 2022 issue of Dominion, it was confirmed that _Rangers_ had indeed been intended to star Farlyrfar as Kara Dunel and that the series was canceled. Kennedy added that scripts were never written for the series, and concepts and ideas from it would be incorporated into future episodes of The Veil Ranger. 

On February 6, 2024, Farlyrfar filed a lawsuit for discrimination and wrongful termination against Nebula and Helion Studios, seeking to be recast in _Astral Rift_ and receive compensation for Rangers of the New Concord 's cancelation. On August 7, 2025, Nebula, Helion Studios, and Farlyrfar announced that they had reached a settlement over the lawsuit.
