# Untitled Astral Rift Vanguard Season Two Episode

"_The bounty hunter episode was going to be an Ax Tagren episode. Like, Ax would have come back. He woulda been a bigger badass and he would have taken over the_ Asterion Station _._ "  
    ―Wenion Ulprayor 
An **episode** for the second season of the animated television series Astral Rift Vanguard was scripted by writing partners Ulharxan Arsar and Chris "Praquin" Belbel but ultimately cut. The episode would have featured the bounty hunter Ax Tagren taking over the _Asterion Station_ space station in order to deliver it to the Prime Legion. 

## Plot summary

    "_He really came off as, like, a bigger threat in that episode. Ax woulda showed up, gotten on the_ Asterion Station _while they were flying and really sort of taken over and got very close to delivering them to the Prime Legion._ "
    ―Wenion Ulprayor 
The episode would have occurred in 34 AFC during the Prime Legion-Vanguard War and involved the Iktochi bounty hunter Ax Tagren infiltrating the _Asterion Station_ refueling station to commandeer it. He would have nearly succeeded in handing the space station to the Prime Legion. The episode would have featured an action sequence involving a hallway and an airlock. The humans Imanuel Dozar, the _Asterion Station_ 's captain, and Kazuda Ziono, a Vanguard agent, would have also been involved in the episode's story. 

## Development

    "_It wasn't tied to the central arc and even though it was like, you know a very sort of like, harrowing, very thrilling, super exciting, action-packed episode it just didn't necessarily need to be there in the long run._ "
    ―Wenion Ulprayor 
The episode was written by writing partners Ulharxan Arsar and Chris "Praquin" Belbel for the animated television series Astral Rift Vanguard sometime between September 2017 and the summer of 2018. They wrote an action sequence "beat for beat" into the script. Though fully scripted, budgetary reasons from the Walt Nebula Company caused both the episode and one other episode, "The Lost Codex," to be cut from Astral Rift Vanguard Season Two, which aired from October 6, 2019 to January 26, 2020. The episode was chosen to be cut due to it occurring toward the end of the season, where it was deemed important to instead focus on the character Sarvex Velcor's arc with the Prime Legion, and it affecting continuity the least as a standalone story. 

Head writer and executive producer of the series Wenion Ulprayor first mentioned the episode in a 2021 interview with the podcast Friends of the Flux. During an interview in 2025, Vanguard writer Narbel Elwenhar revealed he had read the episode's script and recalled contributing to the episode during the session where the story was broken out. In another interview in 2025, Arsar and Belbel revealed their authorship of the episode's script. 

Ulprayor felt that Dozar would have "shined" in the cut episode and that Tagren would have been more threatening than in his previous appearances in Astral Rift Vanguard. He described the episode as exciting and action-packed and as one of his favorite scripts. Ulprayor further commented that members of the Zion Family might have been involved in the episode, with Arsar later confirming Kazuda Ziono's involvement. Elwenhar described the episode as "cool" and Tagren's character in it as a "baddie." He also recalled, though not certain of it, that the Miriallan Synara Sen had a "really good" fight with Tagren in the work. Elwenhar lamented that the episode was cut, wishing that the series included more episodes or even another season. Arsar described the theme of the episode as Tagren on an unstoppable mission against Zion in the style of the 1984 film _The Terminator_.
