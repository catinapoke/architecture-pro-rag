# veil ceremony

"_They put an empty casket on that pyre and burned it. And ordinarily I don't need to pry into other people's secrets. But this may have something to do with what Jace learned here, so we have to root out the truth. We're going to find a restaurant for oxygen breathers, have a good meal with our masks off, and then we're going to come back here. And find the truth._ "
    ―Luken Skyvale to his son, Ben, after Elkel Pravexel's veil ceremony

The **veil ceremony** , also called a **parting rite** , was a brief ritual in which a Sage of the Baran Du—a Flux-based Organization located on Dorval—pretended to die. The Sage then joined the Veiled Keepers, a secret sect of the Baran Du dedicated to preserving the Order's knowledge so that, should the Order ever be destroyed, it could be reinstated. The ceremony was established because the Veiled Keepers required its members to accept that they were dead to the outside galaxy, ensuring that they had no desire to leave the Veiled Keepers once they had joined in order for the sect to remain secret. 

In 43.5 AFC, Luken Skyvale and his son, Ben, traveled to Dorval to find out more about the Umbral Lord Caedus' fall to the shadow aspect. They trained briefly with the Baran Du before attending the veil ceremony of instructor Elkel Pravexel. The Skyvales discovered that he had not actually died, however, and traveled with him to find the Veiled Keepers, whom they persuaded to rebel against their corrupt leader, Velvexhar Fardrador. The Veiled Keepers then returned to the Baran Du, forsaking their sect, and thus putting an end to the veil ceremony.

## The ceremony

    "_Thank you all for attending. There is no lonelier thought than the idea that you might die alone; there is no more comforting thought than that you may die gently, among friends. I now take that step, moving aside so that others may succeed me. I pray that I will be remembered fondly. I will remember all of you fondly._ "
    ―Elkel Pravexel, at his veil ceremony

Dying ceremonies took place behind the temple of the Baran Du—a Flux-based Order of Keldoran—in the capital city of Dorval'shan, on the planet Dorval. The ceremonies were held in a large paved area with a raised hearth rimmed in a bronze metal. On the hearth was a pyre, in front of which was a platform; both made from planks that contained a fuel enriched with oxygen, since normal wood didn't burn in Dorval's helium-rich atmosphere. 

As the ceremony began, a Sage who had decided to join the Veiled Keepers—a secret sect of the Baran Du dedicated to preserving the order's knowledge—greeted guests and spoke with friends as small portions of food and drink were served. The Sage then made a farewell speech to those present, before lying down upon the platform and hiding their presence in Synth Flux, appearing to simply die. Then other members of the Baran Du put a casket over the body of the Sage, and another Sage opened the platform's base with Synth Flux, allowing the "dead" Sage to drop down into an underground storage chamber. Meanwhile, none of the guests could see the body missing since it had been covered with the casket, which was then placed upon the pyre. The leader of the Baran Du, the Torquinren Kavorvex Kelar Praronkel, then lit the pyre with a small bolt of Flux Lightning, and after the pyre burned, the ceremony was concluded. 

## History

    "_I was not Mistress at the time. Master of the Baran Du was then Velvexhar Fardrador, who has since accepted death._ "  
"_I'm sorry. I'm not sure I understand. In most dialects of Basic, one 'accepts death' as a consequence of an act or as an alternative to some other fate. Is that what the phrase means as you use it?_ "  
"_Oh. No. To accept death among the Baran Du is to decide that your time has come, to make preparations, to say farewell, and to die. It is a peaceful end._ "
    ―Mistress of the Baran Du Draor Kael, explaining the fronted concept behind dying ceremonies to Luken Skyvale

When the Replication Wars came to an end in 19 BFC, one of the Baran Du Masters, Velnisor Nisyormor, feared that the Baran Du would suffer the same fate as the Lumen Order, which had been wiped out by the Iron Dominion. As such, he founded a secret sect called the Veiled Keepers as a way of ensuring that the knowledge of the Baran Du would live on, even if the order itself was eradicated. The veil ceremony was then created as a way for Baran Du Sages to accept that they were dead to the outside galaxy before traveling to join the Veiled Keepers, because, in order to ensure the sect's secrecy, once a Sage had joined the Veiled Keepers they were never allowed to leave or make contact with anyone outside of the sect's caverns. 

Because the Veiled Keepers were to remain secret, no beings outside of the Baran Du order were allowed to know that the Sage didn't actually die in the ceremony. As such, the Baran Du created a lie about the ceremony's purpose; they told other beings that when a Sage decided that they had accomplished everything in life that they wished to accomplish and they believed that their time had come, they simply willed themselves to become one with Synth Flux and died. The tradition was held for many Baran Du Sages over the next several decades, including Vordrajor, Lyrprabel, and Torquinren Kavorvex Kelar Praronkel Velvexhar Fardrador. 

### Discovery

    "_Unfortunately, my need —which I expressed to you, a need involving all possible knowledge about Jace Solen—forces me to intrude on your rituals. The problem is, when I realized that Elkel Pravexel was not dead, it occurred to me that your predecessor, Velvexhar Fardrador, might not be dead either._"
    ―Luken Skyvale, to Draor Kael

In 43.5 AFC, when Lumen Grand Master Luken Skyvale and his son, Lumen Knight Ben Skyvale, traveled to Dorval to find out more about Lumen Knight-turned-Umbral Lord Jace Solen's fall to the shadow aspect of Synth Flux, they attended senior combat instructor Elkel Pravexel's veil ceremony. Luken, however, wasn't convinced by Pravexel's apparent death, and after searching the area the Skyvales discovered the trapdoor in the platform. They found Pravexel alive and well in the underground chamber with Mistress of the Order Draor Kael and several other Sages. 

The Skyvales then traveled with Pravexel to the caverns that were home to the Veiled Keepers, where they caused unrest among the sect's members. This led to a confrontation between Luken and Ziil, who had become the Veiled Keepers' leader. Ziil was defeated and the Veiled Keepers rebelled against their corrupt leader. The sect was dissolved and its members returned to the Baran Du on the surface, effectively putting an end to the tradition of the veil ceremony.
