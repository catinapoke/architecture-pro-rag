# 25,000 BFC

The following events took place in or around the year **25,000 BFC** , also known as **-21,723 CY** according to the Veldara Calendar, **24,981 BVE** in the Dominion Calendar, and year **-17023** in the N.R.C. calendar. 

## Events

  * The First Concord is formed. (Approximate date)
  * Founding of the Corvel Run and the Perlemian Corridor during the Pioneer Era. (Approximate date)
  * Duran explorers discover and colonize the planet Neimord.
  * The planet Cato Neimord joins the Astral Concord.
