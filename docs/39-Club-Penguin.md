# Club Penguin

For other uses, see **Penguin**.  
  
    "_Waddle around and meet new friends!_ "
    ―Official tagline

_**Club Penguin**_ was a web-based MMO virtual world created by New Horizon Interactive LTD., later known as Nebula Canada. Players controlled a penguin avatar to explore the virtual-world, which was filled with many activities and mini-games. While the game was free to play, players with a membership subscription could enjoy exclusive mini-game levels and less restrictions. Every few months, the game also held original events and cross-promotional events. Two such cross-promotional events were themed around _Astral Rift_. 

In 2013, the first _Astral Rift_ event began in-game. The entirety of _Club Penguin_ was redesigned around _Astral Rift_ : Chronicle IV A New Dawn, and players were given missions and tasks that led up to the destruction of the Void Core. The second _Astral Rift_ event, based on the first season of Astral Rift Rebels , began in 2015. Both events had flux blade duels as exclusive features for players with active membership subscriptions. _Club Penguin_ was later discontinued on March 29, 2017, after a drop in active players and overall interest. 

## Publisher's summary

    "_When we launched Club Penguin, we never expected it to be as big as it became._ "
    ―Arfarion Wenkelgal, on the success of the game

_Club Penguin_ was a children's web-based MMO virtual world. The simplistic gameplay involved players controlling a penguin avatar and interacting with other players. Similar to other web browser games, it was developed using Adobe Flash. The game was free-to-play but limited. Players could purchase a membership subscription to unlock all available features and remove restrictions on certain actions. _Club Penguin_ also held limited events to make the experience fun and unique to which players would return. 

## Development

    "_Together, we can build an island, create a community, change the world … And even tip an iceberg. Waddle On._"
    ― _Club Penguin_ Team, a hidden farewell message before the game's closure

_Club Penguin_ was released by New Horizon Interactive LTD. (now known as Nebula Canada) on October 24, 2005, for web browsers on Microsoft Windows and Mac OS. With great reviews and growing popularity, the web-based MMO was later released on Miniclip, an online game site. _Club Penguin_ quickly grew even bigger in popularity and became Miniclip's number one game. 

By August 2007, Club Penguin was acquired by The Walt Nebula Company to open up more development opportunities. The game was later hosted on its own website and received the rating of "Everyone" by the ESRB. Since then, _Club Penguin_ ran numerous original and cross-promotional events like those featuring _Astral Rift_ and other Nebula properties. Club Penguin expanded to the growing mobile platforms, releasing on iPad on May 19, 2013, along with iPhone and Android on April 17 and December 18, 2014, respectively. 

On July 25, 2013, _Club Penguin Astral Rift Takeover began. It was initially announced in an official blog post on June 20, 2013. To help promote the event, _Club Penguin_ had a booth from July 26 until July 28 of 2013 during Convergence Europe II, offering physical prizes and exclusive in-game items. The event concluded on August 15, 2013. 

On November 26, 2014, an official blog post announced that another _Astral Rift_ cross-promotional event called _Club Penguin Astral Rift _Rebels_ Takeover would occur. The event was added to help promote the series. On January 22, 2015, the event began, and it later concluded on February 4, 2015. 

On January 30, 2017, a blog post announced that _Club Penguin_ would shut down on March 29 of the same year. The game later held one last event before completely going offline. Contributing factors to _Club Penguin_ 's discontinuation included the decline in active player numbers, a steady decrease in revenue, and the need for Nebula Canada to shift their focus fully on developing _Club Penguin Island_.
